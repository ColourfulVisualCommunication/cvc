# Context — why the platform is shaped this way

Fuller versions of everything here live in the CVC Claude project (*CVC PLATFORM — SCOPE DECISION*, *CVC SERVICE LADDER*, *CVC PLATFORM — BUILD PLAN*). This is the working summary.

## CVC

Colourful Visual Communication — a strategic creative and digital development agency in Ndeiya, Limuru, Kenya. Founded and run by Njoroge, who does both brand identity and full-stack development. That combination is the moat: most brand designers in the market cannot build, and most developers cannot brand.

**Tagline:** From Idea to Action. From Action to Reality.

## The fact that drives every decision

**Every client arrives through a relationship** — a meeting, a WhatsApp message, a phone call from a referral, a friend. Nobody finds CVC through search.

Consequences:

- The site is something you **send**, not something strangers **find**. SEO is not a phase-1 problem, which is why there is no SSR.
- Packaged prices exist as a **sales tool used mid-conversation** ("here's the starter package, KES 30,000, this is what's in it"), not as a self-serve storefront for anonymous buyers.
- The platform's return comes from converting referrals better and getting paid faster, not from generating leads.

## How work reaches CVC today

Physical meetup, WhatsApp, or a referred phone call → define the problem → define scope → quote → deposit → work → invoices and receipts → M-Pesa. It works. The platform is not replacing it.

## The five client journeys

1. **The founder with an idea** — KES 80k–250k, 6–12 weeks. Custom, highest value, needs a call.
2. **The business that needs a logo now** — KES 15k–45k, 1–2 weeks. Packaged, price-sensitive, decides fast.
3. **The event with a fixed date** — KES 40k–150k, immovable deadline. Deposit before any work starts.
4. **The organisation with a committee** — KES 30k–120k, slow approvals, often pays by cheque or transfer. Needs a forwardable quote and manual payment recording.
5. **The client who comes back** — near-zero cost of sale. Should be a large share of revenue. One well-timed post-project nudge is the only automated email worth building.

## What the platform does — seven things

1. A reference point (services, portfolio, blog)
2. A structured enquiry
3. Quotes as links, with expiry and one reminder
4. M-Pesa payments with receipts, plus manual payment recording
5. A project page per client
6. Approvals with a timestamp and a name
7. Six emails

## What stays exactly where it is

Phone calls · WhatsApp all day · meetings · Gmail for personal correspondence.

**Do not build in-app chat.** WhatsApp is the messaging feature.

## The rule that pays for the build

**Final files unlock when the balance clears.** Today, finished work is sent over WhatsApp and payment is asked for afterwards — which is backwards, and where money leaks. The platform inverts it without an awkward conversation: the system holds the file, the payment releases it.

## Domain

`colourfulvisualcommunication.com` — newly registered, **no existing site**. Nothing to migrate, no redirects, no cutover. Phase 2 is a first launch.
